About Herja

Herja is a fantasy Viking RPG inspired by location-based exploration games. The game combines map-style travel, enemies, loot, crafting, settlement building, accounts, friends, clans, chat, and online player presence.

Current Prototype Systems

  • Account login and guest play.
  • Viking and shield maiden character sprites.
  • Enemy combat, XP, gold, and item drops.
  • Inventory, gear, consumables, potion crafting, and building placement.
  • Live multiplayer presence and chat through a WebSocket backend.
  • Friend invites, notifications, and clan basics.

Monetization Plan

Herja plans to use Google-approved game ad formats only at natural breaks, such as map section loading. Gameplay is paused during ad breaks so players are not attacked while an ad is active.
