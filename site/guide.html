How to Play Herja

Use this guide to learn the controls, classes, skill points, items, gear, buildings, messages, friends, clans, dungeons, and multiplayer systems in Herja.

First Steps

Quick Start

1. Choose a Class

Pick Viking, Mage, Druid, or Shield Maiden. Your class controls your starting combat style, class gear, and personal skill tree.

2. Move and Fight

Walk the world, tap enemies, attack them, collect drops, and use potions when your health gets low.

3. Level Up

Enemies, dungeons, Rune Tonics, and rewards give XP. Each level gives 1 skill point for your own class tree.

4. Build and Socialize

Gather resources for buildings, add friends, send messages, join clans, and enter dungeons once you meet the level requirement.

Progression

Skill Points and Skill Trees

How Skill Points Work

You earn exactly 1 skill point for every level gained. Open the skill tree with K, choose a skill from your class tree, and unlock it if you meet the level and prerequisite requirements.

Skill Types

Passive skills improve stats permanently while unlocked. Active skills and special moves can be used from the skill tree and have cooldowns.

Dungeons

Dungeon Entrances and Boss Loot

Entering a Dungeon

Dungeon entrances spawn rarely on the world map and are larger than normal buildings. Walk near one and interact with it. Dungeons start at level 10 and unlock in tiers every 10 levels.

Level Requirements

If a dungeon says Level 20 Required, your character must be at least level 20. If you are too low, the game shows the required level message.

Inside the Dungeon

Dungeons load a separate smaller map with boundaries, harder enemies, better XP, better gold, better drops, and a boss near the end or center.

Boss Rewards

Boss loot is individual. Eligible players get their own reward roll, including gold, XP, useful items, and a chance at rare or legendary class-matching weapons.

Social

Friends, Invites, Messages, and Notifications

Friends

Open the social menu with O to manage friends. Friend systems are used for invites, player organization, and future group features.

Messages

The social menu includes messaging and notifications so players can coordinate while exploring, building, fighting, and preparing for clan activity.

Notifications

Watch the HUD and social screens for clear messages about invites, clan activity, errors, dungeon requirements, rewards, and battle updates.

Multiplayer Presence

Nearby players can appear in the world. Herja is being expanded toward MMO-style social play with friends, clans, and group rewards.

Clans

Clans, Perks, Wars, and Battles

Create ClanCosts 10,000 gold. The creator becomes leader and chooses one starting perk.
Clan SizeA clan can hold up to 100 members.
PerksXP Boost, Gold Boost, Hit Bonus, Defense Bonus, or Boss Hunter. Perks apply only while you are in the clan.
RolesLeader, Officer, and Member control clan management permissions.
WarsLeaders can challenge other clans. Wars can be pending, accepted, active, completed, or cancelled.
Scheduled BattlesClan leaders can schedule clan-vs-clan battles. Participating players earn individual rewards.

Tips

Good Early Game Route

Fight low-level enemies, collect every item drop, craft potions, build a campfire, spend skill points as you level, save gold for clan creation, and start dungeons at level 10.
