Game Features

Explore

Move through a section-loaded world inspired by real map coordinates, with region transitions built for mobile and browser play.

Build

Gather wood, stone, herbs, ore, and magical materials to place useful Viking structures across your travels.

Multiplayer

See nearby players, chat in real time, manage friends and invites, and organize around clans.

Herja is in active prototype development. Accounts, saves, multiplayer presence, chat, crafting, and building systems are being expanded regularly.
