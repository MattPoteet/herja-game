Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Herja collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit herjagame.com or play Herja.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as email address, player name, character choice, friends, clan, and login/session data.
  • Gameplay information, such as level, XP, health, gold, inventory, buildings, chat messages, multiplayer presence, and save progress.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, and approximate diagnostics.
  • Optional map/location-related information if location features are enabled. Prototype map movement may also use simulated coordinates.

How We Use Information

  • To provide accounts, saves, multiplayer, chat, friends, clans, and game progress.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the website and game.
  • To prevent abuse, cheating, fraud, spam, and unauthorized access.
  • To serve ads and measure ad performance where advertising is enabled.

Cookies, Ads, and Google Services

Herja may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies for site operation, analytics, fraud prevention, and advertising.

Third parties, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers to collect information as a result of ad serving on this website or game page.

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can manage Google ad personalization at Google Ad Settings.

Legal Bases and Consent

Where required, Herja will request consent before using personalized advertising, analytics, or location-related features. If you decline consent, some features or personalized ads may be limited.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate hosting, authentication, databases, multiplayer, security, analytics, and advertising. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect players, the game, or the public.

Children

Herja is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or play if you are under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Data Retention

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active or as needed for operation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, security, and backups.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of account data. Some information may be retained if needed for security, legal compliance, or legitimate business records.
